Hearsay, campfire talk, half-remembered history

The Legend of Veyrholm

No two accounts of the burned city agree. These are only the stories told above ground — the truth is still down there.

I

The City

Veyrholm stood for generations — prosperous, warm through the worst winters, sure of itself. The travelers' songs remember it as beautiful. The songs remember everything as beautiful.

II

The Fire

Then it burned. Ask ten storytellers how it started and you will hear ten different causes, told with equal certainty. All that is agreed upon is the ash.

III

The Treasure

Every ruin grows a legend. Veyrholm's is a vault beneath the foundations, and in it a relic of immense worth — though every teller names the prize differently.

IV

The Crack

For centuries the vault kept itself shut, and the stories stayed stories. This season, the ground shifted. The way down stands open for the first time in living memory.

Relic hunter's field manual

Type what you'd do

The parser meets you halfway. No verb lists to memorize — if it reads like an instruction, it probably works.

look north take torch use torch inspect torch read inscription use … on … combine … with … inventory lore hint save
  • Keyboard-first. Arrow keys recall command history; the prompt keeps focus. Quick-command buttons exist, but the whole game plays from the keys.
  • Nothing is missable by accident. Required items can't be dropped, and failed attempts never jam a mechanism for good.
  • Accessible by design. High-contrast text, semantic markup, a live transcript, and an Atmosphere toggle that stills all motion.
